# EDR-RC-CAR-0001: Four Geared Motors

## Context

The radio-controlled car needs simple traction, predictable wiring, and maintainable motor replacement.

## Decision

Use four geared motors near the wheels for the first engineering prototype.

## Consequences

Battery sizing, transmission layout, and driver selection must be reviewed when the motors change.
