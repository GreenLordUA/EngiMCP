# Radio-Controlled Car Specification v0

## Purpose

Radio-controlled vehicle for outdoor driving and engineering experiments.

## Requirements

- Runtime: at least 30 minutes under mixed driving.
- Radio control: stable control link within the operating area.
- Chassis: 4x4.
- Drive: 4 geared motors near the wheels.
- Verification: runtime must be tested with the selected motors and battery.
