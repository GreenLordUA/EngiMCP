# Radio-Controlled Car Motors

## Motor Choice

The project motors are four geared DC motors selected for outdoor traction and low-speed control.

## Engineering Notes

Changing the motors affects stall current, battery capacity, transmission ratio, driver thermal margin, and the motor decision record.
